Jumbo

Don is a boy everyone calls Jumbo because of his size. When a ghost from another world asks for his help, he and his best friends Mae and Nurman set off on an adventure guided by the old storybook his parents left behind. I built environments for the film at Afterlab Studio under Visinema Studios, then maintained its assets at Visinema through the theatrical release.
